Kleenest Community Guidelines

Effective: September 1, 2026

Kleenest is a trust network. Contributions should help the next person understand a restroom, location, business or experience more accurately.

Be truthful

Share genuine observations and experiences. Do not fabricate visits, reviews, photos, accessibility claims, cleanliness reports, amenities, check-ins, votes or evidence. Do not coordinate rating manipulation or exchange incentives for deceptive content.

Be safe and respectful

No threats, harassment, stalking, hate, dehumanizing abuse, sexual exploitation, non-consensual sexual content, glorification of violence, or instructions intended to cause harm.

Protect privacy

Do not expose private personal information, credentials, private communications or precise personal location information without authorization. Avoid identifiable photos of people when they are not necessary to document the restroom or business context.

No scams, spam or impersonation

Do not impersonate people or businesses, phish for credentials, distribute malware, run deceptive promotions, spam users, or use community features for unrelated solicitation.

Relevant contributions

Keep reviews, comments, photos and reports relevant to the place, restroom or community interaction. Business disputes should not be used to retaliate against unrelated contributors.

Reporting and blocking

Use in-app Report controls for content or behavior that may violate these Guidelines. Use Block to stop direct interaction with a contributor. Reports are reviewed by authorized Trust & Safety workflows and may be combined with verification and abuse-prevention signals.

Enforcement

Kleenest may remove content, reduce distribution, invalidate manipulated rewards, restrict functionality, suspend accounts, or preserve evidence depending on severity, history, risk, and applicable law. Good-faith mistakes are treated differently from deliberate abuse.

Appeals and support

If you believe a moderation decision is wrong, contact Help & Support in the app with relevant context.
