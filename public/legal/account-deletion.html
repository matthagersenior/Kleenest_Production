Delete a Kleenest account

This resource applies to the Kleenest consumer app and account.

Fastest method: in the app

  1. Sign in to Kleenest.
  2. Open Profile.
  3. Open Account control.
  4. Follow the account-deletion confirmation steps.

The in-app flow submits the deletion request directly to Kleenest while you are authenticated.

If you cannot access the app

Use the developer contact shown on the Kleenest Google Play/App Store listing and state that you are requesting Kleenest account deletion. Include the email address associated with the account. Do not send your password. Kleenest may require reasonable verification before deleting an account to prevent unauthorized deletion.

What is deleted

The deletion workflow removes or de-identifies account data according to the Kleenest deletion process, including account/profile data and personal data that no longer needs to be retained.

What may be retained

Limited records may be retained when necessary for fraud prevention, abuse and security investigations, financial/accounting obligations, dispute resolution, legal requirements, or de-identified network integrity. Retained information is restricted to those purposes and applicable retention periods.

Timing

Deletion requests are processed through Kleenest's account-deletion workflow. Some backups or legally retained records may age out on a different schedule than active account data.
