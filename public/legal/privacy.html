Kleenest Privacy Policy

Effective: September 1, 2026

Kleenest is a restroom discovery, trust, community, business, and operations platform. This policy describes the information Kleenest processes when you use its apps and services.

Information we process

Depending on the features you use, Kleenest may process account and profile information; device and app-use information; approximate or precise location; check-ins and arrival evidence; saved locations and routes; reviews, photos and other community contributions; social relationships and messages; notification tokens and preferences; QR interactions; progression, rewards, contests and campaign activity; business or fleet operational information; support requests; safety reports; moderation records; and technical diagnostics.

Location and Live Network

Location is used for features such as nearby restroom discovery, routing, verified arrival or check-in, Live Network, geofencing, operational dispatch, and related notifications. Background location is used only when a feature that requires it is enabled and the operating system has granted permission. You can change location permissions in device settings.

How information is used

Kleenest uses information to provide requested features; maintain restroom and business data; personalize discovery and progression; verify contributions; prevent fraud and abuse; provide notifications; operate business, Fleet and Owner control surfaces; support users; improve reliability; enforce policies; satisfy legal obligations; and protect users and the platform.

Public and community information

Information you intentionally publish, such as contributor profile fields, reviews, ratings, photos and other community contributions, may be visible to other users. Private account credentials, private safety reports and restricted operational data are not made public merely because you use community features.

AI features

Kleenest AI may process the Kleenest context and instructions supplied for an AI request. AI output is advisory and can be reported in-app. Reported AI responses may be retained with trace and moderation information for safety review.

Sharing and service providers

Kleenest may use infrastructure, authentication, mapping, notification, analytics, hosting, storage and other service providers to operate the service. Information is shared only as necessary for those functions, legal obligations, safety, fraud prevention, corporate transactions, or with your direction. Kleenest does not publish private conversations to advertisers.

Retention and deletion

Retention depends on the data type, account status, product requirements, security needs and legal obligations. Account deletion can be requested in the app or through the account-deletion resource linked below. Some records may be retained where necessary for fraud prevention, security, disputes, legal compliance, accounting, or de-identified network integrity.

Your controls

You can manage profile information, permissions, notification settings, community blocking, account preferences, and account deletion from the app. Device-level permissions can also be changed in your operating system settings.

Children

Kleenest consumer accounts are not intended to be used in violation of applicable age-consent requirements. Family or supervised experiences, where offered, are subject to their specific controls and applicable law.

Security

Kleenest uses access controls, server-side authorization, audit records, encryption provided by platform infrastructure, and abuse-prevention controls. No service can guarantee absolute security.

Changes

Material policy changes may require renewed in-app acceptance. The current policy version is displayed in the app.

Contact

Use the Help & Support area in Kleenest or the developer contact shown on the Kleenest store listing for privacy questions.
