Kleenest Privacy Policy

Effective: September 5, 2026

Kleenest is a restroom discovery, trust, community, business, fleet, and platform-operations service. This policy describes the information Kleenest processes when you use the Kleenest Consumer, Business, Fleet, or KleenestOS apps and related services.

Information we process

Depending on the app and features you use, Kleenest may process account and profile information; device and app-use information; approximate or precise location; check-ins and arrival evidence; saved locations and routes; reviews, photos and other community contributions; social relationships and messages; notification tokens and preferences; QR interactions; progression, rewards, contests and campaign activity; business or fleet operational information; support requests; safety reports; moderation records; and technical diagnostics.

Location and Live Network

Kleenest uses location for user-facing features such as nearby restroom discovery, routing, verified arrival or check-in, Live Network, geofencing, operational dispatch, and related notifications. The Consumer, Business, and Fleet apps can request background location only for an optional feature that needs it and only after the user enables that feature, receives a prominent in-app explanation, and grants the relevant Android permission. KleenestOS does not request background location in the current release.

Consumer: if you enable Consumer Live Network, Kleenest may use location while the app is closed or not in use so Android can monitor nearby restroom regions and provide eligible nearby-restroom alerts.

Business: if an authorized operator enables Business Live Network, Kleenest may use location while the app is closed or not in use so Android can monitor active Business location geofences, record eligible operational enter/exit events, and support related alerts.

Fleet: if an authorized operator enables Fleet Live Network for an active route, Kleenest may use location while the app is closed or not in use so Android can monitor route-stop geofences, detect eligible operational arrivals/departures, and support route alerts.

Background location is not used for advertising. Live Network can be disabled in the relevant app, and device location permissions can be changed in Android settings.

How information is used

Kleenest uses information to provide requested features; maintain restroom and business data; personalize discovery and progression; verify contributions; prevent fraud and abuse; provide notifications; operate business, Fleet and Owner control surfaces; support users; improve reliability; enforce policies; satisfy legal obligations; and protect users and the platform.

Public and community information

Information you intentionally publish, such as contributor profile fields, reviews, ratings, photos and other community contributions, may be visible to other users. Private account credentials, private safety reports and restricted operational data are not made public merely because you use community features.

AI features

Kleenest AI may process Kleenest context and instructions supplied for an AI request. AI output is advisory and can be reported in-app. Reported AI responses may be retained with trace and moderation information for safety review.

Sharing and service providers

Kleenest may use infrastructure, authentication, mapping, notification, analytics, hosting, storage and other service providers to operate the service. Information is shared only as necessary for those functions, legal obligations, safety, fraud prevention, corporate transactions, or with your direction. Kleenest does not publish private conversations to advertisers and does not sell background location for advertising.

Retention and deletion

Retention depends on the data type, account status, product requirements, security needs and legal obligations. Consumer account deletion can be requested in the app or through the account-deletion resource linked below. Some records may be retained where necessary for fraud prevention, security, disputes, legal compliance, accounting, or de-identified network integrity. Business, Fleet, and KleenestOS access may also be governed by organization or platform administration.

Your controls

You can manage profile information, permissions, notification settings, community blocking, account preferences, Live Network state, and applicable account-deletion controls from Kleenest. Device-level permissions can also be changed in your operating system settings.

Children

Kleenest consumer accounts are not intended to be used in violation of applicable age-consent requirements. Family or supervised experiences, where offered, are subject to their specific controls and applicable law.

Security

Kleenest uses access controls, server-side authorization, audit records, encryption provided by platform infrastructure, and abuse-prevention controls. No service can guarantee absolute security.

Changes

Material policy changes may require renewed in-app acceptance. The current policy version is displayed in the app.

Contact

Use the Help & Support area in Kleenest or the developer contact shown on the applicable Kleenest Google Play store listing for privacy questions.
